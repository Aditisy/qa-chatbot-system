Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology company. Microsoft was founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen on April 4, 1975. The company is headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Microsoft is best known for developing the Windows operating system and the Microsoft Office suite of productivity software. Satya Nadella has served as the Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft since 2014. Microsoft is one of the largest technology companies in the world by revenue and market capitalization.
