The theory of relativity was developed by Albert Einstein in the early twentieth century. Einstein published the special theory of relativity in 1905 and the general theory of relativity in 1915. The theory fundamentally changed the understanding of space, time, and gravity. Albert Einstein was born in Ulm, Germany in 1879. He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his explanation of the photoelectric effect, not for relativity itself. Einstein's work laid the foundation for modern physics and influenced later discoveries such as black holes and gravitational waves.
