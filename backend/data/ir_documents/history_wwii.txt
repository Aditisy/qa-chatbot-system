World War II began in 1939 when Germany invaded Poland. The war involved most of the world's nations and lasted until 1945. The war ended in Europe on May 8, 1945, a date known as Victory in Europe Day. Japan surrendered on September 2, 1945, after the United States dropped atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Winston Churchill served as the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom during most of the war. The war caused the deaths of an estimated 70 to 85 million people, making it the deadliest conflict in human history.
