Natural Language Processing, commonly abbreviated as NLP, is a subfield of artificial intelligence focused on the interaction between computers and human language. NLP techniques are used in applications such as machine translation, sentiment analysis, and question answering systems. Common NLP tasks include part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and text classification. Modern NLP systems often rely on machine learning models, including transformer-based architectures such as BERT and GPT. Universities around the world now offer dedicated courses in Natural Language Processing as part of artificial intelligence curricula.
