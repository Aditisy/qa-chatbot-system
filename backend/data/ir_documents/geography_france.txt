France is a country located in Western Europe. Paris is the capital and largest city of France. The Eiffel Tower, located in Paris, was completed in 1889 and designed by engineer Gustave Eiffel. France has a population of approximately 68 million people as of recent estimates. The official language of France is French. France is known for its rich culture, cuisine, and history, and it shares borders with countries including Germany, Spain, Italy, and Belgium.
